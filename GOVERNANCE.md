# Governance

The v0.1 repository is maintainer-governed while the specification is unstable.

Schema changes should be accompanied by:

- a clear rationale;
- compatibility impact;
- updated examples/fixtures;
- validation updates where necessary;
- an Architecture Decision Record for decisions that materially affect long-term corpus semantics.

Tradition-specific authority or normative ranking belongs to consumer policy profiles, not the canonical corpus core.
