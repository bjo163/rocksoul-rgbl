# Gathas of Zarathustra (Gāthās / Yasna) — Complete 17 Hymns

The 17 sacred poetic hymns composed by the prophet Zarathustra Spitama in Old Avestan, forming the core spiritual heart of the Zoroastrian Avesta.

## Coverage
- Complete 17 hymns across the 5 Gāthās:
  1. Gāthā Ahunavaitī (Yasna 28–34, 7 hymns)
  2. Gāthā Ushtavaitī (Yasna 43–46, 4 hymns)
  3. Gāthā Spəntā Mainyū (Yasna 47–50, 4 hymns)
  4. Gāthā Vohū Khshathra (Yasna 51, 1 hymn)
  5. Gāthā Vahishtōishti (Yasna 53, 1 hymn)
- Each hymn includes:
  - Original Avestan text in standard Romanized transcription (`ae`)
  - Complete English translation (`en`)
  - Complete Indonesian translation (`id`)
  - Yasna chapter and Gatha taxonomy metadata
