# Authentic Islamic Duas Collection

Curated dataset of 126 authentic supplications (Duas & Adhkar) from the Quran and Sunnah across 27 life categories.

## Coverage
- 126 authentic duas across 27 thematic categories (Morning, Evening, Sleep, Wudu, Prayer, Travel, Gratitude, Distress, etc.)
- Each dua includes:
  - Arabic text with full vocalization (harakat)
  - English translation
  - Phonetic Latin transliteration
  - Category identifier & description

## Sources & Provenance
- Source: Classical prophetic traditions (Hisn al-Muslim, Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, Sunan at-Tirmidhi) and Quranic supplications.
- Ingestion endpoint: UmmahAPI Authentic Duas API.
- Rights: CC0-1.0 Public Domain.

## App Target
- Maps to `wx.dua` and `wx.devotional` in MoonWitness app.
