# Tao Te Ching (Daodejing / 道德經) — Complete 81 Chapters

Foundational philosophical and spiritual classic of Taoism attributed to Laozi (Lao Tzu), composed during the Spring and Autumn / Warring States period (6th–4th century BCE).

## Coverage
- Complete 81 chapters:
  - Part 1: Tao Ching (Chapters 1–37, The Book of the Way)
  - Part 2: Te Ching (Chapters 38–81, The Book of Virtue)
- Each chapter includes:
  - Classical Chinese text in standard Traditional Chinese characters (`lzh`)
  - Complete English translation (James Legge, 1891 / Public Domain)
  - Indonesian thematic title & contextual summary
