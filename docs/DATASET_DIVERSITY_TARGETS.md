# Dataset diversity target plan

P14 expands the corpus only through source-pinned, rights-aware, reviewable lanes. It measures diversity across traditions, source languages, genres, communities, historical periods, and textual scales; a larger record count alone is not diversity.

## Layer boundaries

Primary/source text, translation, commentary, transmission report, ritual/manual, biography, and modern reference data are separate products. A translation never replaces source text. A commentary never becomes the primary text. A person, tradition, or role claim must remain a P13 entity/assertion with provenance.

## Text-scale policy

The corpus supports complete works where rights and source quality permit, and bounded excerpts/fragments otherwise. Every fragment must retain its parent work/edition, citation scheme, selector or passage range, source artifact, rights basis, and a statement of completeness. A fragment is never silently presented as a complete scripture.

## Community and ethics boundary

Indigenous, oral, ceremonial, initiatory, restricted, living-community, and culturally sensitive material is discovery/audit-first. Absence of a technical download barrier is not sufficient permission to bundle. The default is metadata-only until a rights and community-context review explicitly permits a source-preserving dataset.

## Exit criteria

P14 is complete only when its matrix demonstrates diversity by tradition, genre, source language, `en`/`id` coverage, source rights state, text scale, and unresolved gaps. It must not imply that the included set is exhaustive or representative of every community.
