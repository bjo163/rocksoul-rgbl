# P16 — Devotional, prayer, liturgy, and practice corpus

P16 stores prayers, hymns, mantras, blessings, creeds, chants, liturgies, ritual instructions, observances, and practice descriptions as sourced textual or structured records. A prayer text, ritual practice, and community claim are different layers. Descriptive metadata must not instruct users to perform restricted, initiatory, hazardous, or culturally sensitive rites.

Each text retains work, edition, language, citation, completeness, community/tradition scope, rights, provenance, and translation lineage. Audio and performance recordings remain separate media artifacts. Variants are edition- or community-specific, not silently merged.

P16 exits only with diverse, rights-safe coverage and consent-aware handling of living or restricted traditions.
