# P17 — Intertextual evidence and research graph

P17 links P12–P16 records through reviewable evidence: mentions, quotations, citations, commentary targets, parallel passages, motifs, events, places, roles, and transmission relations. A link states what a source or curator asserts; it does not prove identity, dependence, historicity, or theological equivalence.

Machine-assisted extraction may create candidates only. Canonical graph edges require method, confidence/status, provenance, and review state. Negative, uncertain, disputed, and unmatched results are retained rather than hidden.

P17 exits with deterministic graph snapshots, explanation paths from every assertion to exact evidence, benchmark queries, reconciliation reports, and no opaque machine-generated canonical claims.
