# P13 World Religions & Persons Registry v0.1

Status: bounded, reproducible baseline plus seven exact textual-evidence links. The registry contains 164 baseline records, including 10 explicit unresolved-historicity assessments, and the evidence layer contains 9 metadata records. Wikidata QIDs, revision metadata, and SHA-256s are retained in the pinned snapshot manifest.

The exit gates pass for scoped roles, bounded/non-final taxonomy, and identity records carrying external IDs. Automatic duplicate merges are disabled. Wikidata preferred labels, aliases, and native/source-language labels are retained where present. Ten explicit `historicity_unresolved` assessments now preserve uncertainty as separate records; broader scholarly historicity evidence remains an explicit gap in `datasets/p13-coverage.json`.

Exact evidence currently links Quran 2:87 and 33:40, WLC Exodus 3:1 and Genesis 12:1, and SBLGNT 1 Corinthians 1:1. Source text is not duplicated by the P13 layer; dependency manifests govern its rights.
