# P15 multilingual lexicon coverage report

The current P15 release contains two supplemental, source-scoped seeds: Quran Arabic and bilingual Dhammapada English–Indonesian. They contain 24 records, 5 concepts, 5 terms, and 7 exact passage-usage evidence records. Native/source forms, access-language forms, and transliterations remain separate records or forms.

The seed passes scoped-concept, source-evidence, and no-unsourced-definition gates. It intentionally does not claim a universal religious dictionary or translation-only equivalence. Full language/tradition expansion and attributed human definitions remain open; machine-generated glosses are not canonical.
