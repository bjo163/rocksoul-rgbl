# P15 — Multilingual religious lexicon and concept registry

P15 models terms and concepts separately. A term is a language/script-specific lexical form; a concept is a source-scoped meaning candidate. Similar translations do not establish cross-tradition equivalence. Sacred names, epithets, titles, and honorifics remain distinguishable from the P13 entities they may denote.

Definitions, etymologies, broader/narrower relations, and cross-tradition comparisons require exact provenance. Native script is primary where available; transliteration is a derived representation with scheme and version. `en` and `id` labels improve access but never replace source-language forms.

P15 exits only when high-priority concepts have multilingual coverage, passage/source evidence, deterministic reconciliation, explicit contested/ambiguous states, and no translation-only equivalence.
