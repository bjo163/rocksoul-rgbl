# Bibliographic profile

`bibliographic.record` describes a work, expression, edition, article, or other scholarly item and its publication/citation metadata. Authors and organizations are canonical entity references; authority identifiers remain external identifiers. Dates, titles, publishers, and edition statements are preserved as descriptive source data and do not imply authenticity, authority, or scholarly quality.

Bibliographic records may point to a source/provenance record and may carry evidence references. Multiple records can describe competing catalog records for the same work; reconciliation is explicit and never inferred from title similarity alone.
