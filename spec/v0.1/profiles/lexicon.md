# Lexicon profile 0.1

`lexicon@0.1` keeps lexical forms, source-scoped concepts, definitions, usage evidence, and P13 entities separate. A spelling, shared translation, similar function, or cross-tradition name match is never evidence of identity or equivalence by itself.

Concept and term records require provenance. Definitions, usage, relations, etymologies, and designations require explicit scope and/or certainty. Sacred names, epithets, titles, and honorifics point to an existing entity through a provenance-bearing designation resource; they do not create, merge, or reconcile that entity. Transliteration records declare scheme/version and lossiness. Sensitive or disputed terminology is represented with reviewable state flags rather than silently normalized.

Automated matching may produce candidates only. Promotion to an asserted relation requires human review, source evidence, and the narrowest defensible tradition/community/work/passage/period/language scope.
