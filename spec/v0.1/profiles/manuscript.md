# Manuscript and folio profile

`manuscript.witness` records a physical or digital witness, shelfmark, folio/side, and line range. The profile keeps the witness structure separate from textual content and does not assume that folio numbering, pagination, lineation, or sigla are globally comparable.

Repository, collection, shelfmark, folio, side, and line values preserve the source's notation. A manuscript witness may link to a textual target, but that link does not assert that the witness is the text's only or authoritative realization. IIIF references are defined by the later media/artifact profile.
